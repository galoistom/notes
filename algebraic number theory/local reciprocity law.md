>[!note] Theorem 
>For every nonarchimedian local field $K$, there exists a unique homomorphism $\phi_{K}:K^{\times}\rightarrow Gal(K^{ab}/K)$, with the following properties:
>1. for every prime element $\pi$ of $K$ and every finite unraminfied extension $L$ of $K$, $\phi_{K}(\pi)$ acts on $L$ as $Forb_{L/K}$;
>2. for every finite abelian extension $L$ of $K$, $Nm_{L /K}(L^{\times})$ is contained in the kernel of $a\mapsto \phi_{K}(a)|L$, and $\phi_{K}$ induces and isomorphism $\phi_{L /K}:K^{\times} /Nm_{L /K}(L^{\times})\rightarrow Gal(L /K)$. In particular, $(K^{\times}:Nm_{L /K}(L^{\times}))=[L:K]$. Denote $Nm_{L /K}(L^{\times})$ by $Nm(L^{\times})$.

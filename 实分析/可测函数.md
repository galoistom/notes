
>[!note] 定义
>一个函数$f: E \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \cup \{ +\infty, -\infty \}$ 是可测函数如果对任意$e \in \mathbb{R} \cup \{ +\infty, -\infty \}$ 有$E(f \geq e):=\{x : f(x) \geq e\}$ 可测（c.f.[[测度]]）.

我们很自然的去考虑用一列好的函数$f_n$去逼近一个可测函数$f$, 那如何刻画函数列的收敛?

>[!note] 依测度收敛
>设$f_1,f_2 \cdots$ 是定义在$E$上的一个几乎处处有限的可测函数列, $f$ 是$E$上几乎处处有限的可测的函数, 称函数列依测度收敛于$f$若对任一正数$\sigma$, $\lim\limits_{n \rightarrow \infty}[m^*(f-f_n)>\sigma]=0$

下面是一些简单性质:
1. 若函数$\{f_n(x)\}$依测度收敛于 $f(x)$, 则必有子函数列 $f_{n_1}(x), f_{n_2}(x), f_{n_3}(x), \cdots$ 几乎处处收敛于 $f(x).$
2. 设可测集 $E$ 上 已给一列处处有限的可测函数:$f_1(x),f_2(x),f_3(x), \cdots$, 且它们几乎处处收敛于几乎处处有限的函数$f(x)$, $\lim\limits_{n \rightarrow \infty}f_n=f$.在此假设下, 对于任一正数$\delta$, 存在如下的可测集$E_{\delta} \subset E:$ 使得$mE_{\delta}>mE-\delta$, 且在$E_\delta$上, $f_n(x)$ 一致收敛于$f(x)$.

we have the following theorem about approximation:

**Theorem**:
Suppose $f$ is a non-negative measurable function on $\mathbb{R}^d$. Then there exists an increasiong sequence of non-negative simple function $\{\phi_k\}_{k=1}^\infty$ that converges pointwise to $f$, namely, $\phi_k(x) \leq \phi_{k+1}(x)$ and $\lim\limits_{k \rightarrow \infty}\phi_k(x) f(x)$

*Proof*:
for $N \geq 1$, let $Q_N$ denote the cube contered at the origin and of the size length $N$. 
Then take $F_N(x)=\begin{cases}f(x) \ &\text{ if } x \in Q_N \text{ and } f(x) \leq N,\\ N &\text{ if } x \in Q_N \text{ and } f(x)>N,\\ 0 &\text{ otherwise}\end{cases}$
one can sasily varify that the $F_N$'s are what we desired.

We can easily extend the theorem to general measurable functions:

**Theorem**:
Suppose $f$ is measurable on $\mathbb{R}^d$. Then there exists a sequence of simple functions $\{\phi_k\}_{k=1}^\infty$ that satifies $|\phi_k(x)|\leq |\phi_{k+1}(x)|$ and $\lim\limits_{k \rightarrow \infty}\phi_k(x)=f(x)$ for all $x$.

>[!note] Littlewood's three principle
>1. Every set is nearly a finite union of intervals.
>2. Every function is nearly continuous.
>3. Every convergent sequence is nearly uniformly convergent.

**Theorem**(Egorov):
Suppose $\{f_k\}_{k=1}^\infty$ is a sequence of measurable functions defined on a measurable set $E$ given $\epsilon>0$, we can find a closed set $A_\epsilon \subset E$ such that $m(E-A_\epsilon) \leq \epsilon$ and $f_k \rightarrow f$ uniformly on $A_\epsilon$.

*Proof*:
We may assume without loss of generality that $f_k(x) \rightarrow f(x)$ for every $x \in E$. For each pair of non-negative integers $n$ and $k$, let $E_k^n=\{x \in E: |f_j(x)-f(x)|<\frac{1}{n},\ \forall j>k\}$. By construction, we then have $|f_j(x)-f(x)|<\frac{1}{n}$ whenever $j>k_n$ and $x \in E_{k_n}^n$. We choose $N$ large enough, then we observe that $m(E-\tilde{A}_\epsilon) < \frac{\epsilon}{2}$. As a result, we have $m(E-A_\epsilon)<\epsilon$ and the theorem is proved.
